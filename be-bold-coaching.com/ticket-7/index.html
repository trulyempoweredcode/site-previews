How We Work Together

Real change needs more than insight

You’ve read the books and listened to the podcasts. What you need now isn’t more information, it’s space to slow down, understand yourself properly, and stop carrying everything alone. Unlike traditional therapy alone, my work combines emotional healing with practical change, helping you understand the roots of your struggles while building a different way of living, across three gentle stages.

1
Understand

Make sense of what’s really going on

Together we explore why you overthink, why rest feels uncomfortable, and why you feel responsible for everyone — the patterns quietly shaping how you feel.

2
Shift

Understand what needs to change

Through nervous-system regulation and reflection, we loosen the old beliefs and create a different, kinder relationship with yourself in the present.

3
Live

Step into a new way of living

With support and accountability, you start practising new ways of responding — so life begins to feel lighter, calmer and more like your own.

The Six-Month Container

Why I work over six months

Most of the struggles my clients bring have been developing for years, sometimes decades. While insight can happen quickly, lasting change usually takes longer. A six-month container gives us the time to build trust, understand what’s really happening beneath the surface, and create meaningful change that lasts beyond our sessions together.
