What Happens Next

Reaching out is the hardest part

If you’re used to coping alone, getting in touch can feel like a big step. Here’s exactly what to expect, gentle, and at your pace.

1
Step One

You send a message

Fill in the form or email me directly. You don’t need the right words or to have everything figured out, a few lines is plenty.

2
Step Two

We have a free consultation

A relaxed, no-pressure conversation to explore what you’re struggling with and how I work. There’s plenty of space for your questions too.

3
Step Three

You decide what feels right

If it feels like a good fit, we’ll plan your next steps together. If not, there’s absolutely no obligation to continue.

There’s nothing weak about needing support

Sometimes healing begins simply by allowing yourself to stop coping in silence. Whenever you’re ready, I’m here.
